CHIPS Alliance was proud to sponsor Latch-Up 2026 , held May 1-3, 2026, in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. Organized by the FOSSi Foundation , Latch-Up is a weekend of presentations and networking dedicated to free and open source silicon. Designed for the open source digital design community, the event brings together engineers, developers, and contributors to share technical work and connect across the ecosystem.
