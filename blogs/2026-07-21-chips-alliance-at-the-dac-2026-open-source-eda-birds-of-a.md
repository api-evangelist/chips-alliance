CHIPS Alliance will participate in the “Open-Source EDA, Data and Collaboration” Birds-of-a-Feather session at DAC 2026 on Tuesday, July 28, at the Long Beach Convention Center. The seventh in a series that began at DAC 2018, the BoF will bring together academic researchers, industry practitioners, and open source maintainers for rapid-fire presentations, dedicated Q&A panels, and networking opportunities.
