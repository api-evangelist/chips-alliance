CHIPS Alliance’s latest Deep Dive Cafe featured an outstanding talk by a Google physical design engineer, Young-Joon Lee, who has a PhD from Georgia Tech, and has been working on machine learning physical design projects for the past two years. The chip placement problem is a notoriously challenging design problem, and has been explored in the electronic design automation research and development community for years.
