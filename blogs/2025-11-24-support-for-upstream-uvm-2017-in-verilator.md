<p><a href="https://www.accellera.org/community/uvm">Universal Verification Methodology (UVM)</a> is one of the most popular verification methods in digital design, focusing on standardization and reusability of verification IP and environments. For the last few years, Antmicro has been gradually completing milestones toward full UVM support in the open source <a href="https://github.com/verilator">Verilator RTL simulator</a>, from <a href="https://antmicro.com/blog/2021/05/dynamic-scheduling-in-verilator/">dynamic scheduling</a> (later enhanced with <a href="https://antmicro.com/blog/2021/12/coroutines-for-dynamic-scheduling-in-verilator/">coroutines</a>) through a <a href="https://antmicro.com/blog/2023/10/running-simple-uvm-testbenches-in-verilator/">proof-of-concept UVM testbench running in Verilator</a> to <a href="https://antmicro.com/blog/2024/03/introducing-constrained-randomization-in-verilator/">constrained randomization</a>.</p>
<p>With this article, CHIPS Alliance is happy to announce that Verilator can now elaborate upstream UVM 2017-1.0 - <a href="https://github.com/chipsalliance/uvm-verilator/pull/9">no patches or workarounds</a> required. This important milestone was spearheaded by Antmicro&rsquo;s efforts within <a href="https://www.chipsalliance.org/">CHIPS Alliance</a> and complemented by the work of Wilson Snyder, the maintainer of Verilator, as well as the broader open source community.</p>
