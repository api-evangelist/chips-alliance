<h2 id="how-a-globally-distributed-program-manager-is-helping-define-the-next-phase-of-open-source-silicon-collaboration">How a globally distributed program manager is helping define the next phase of open source silicon collaboration.</h2>
<p><strong>Introduction</strong></p>
<p>Aaron Cunningham serves as Chair of the CHIPS Alliance Technical Advisory Council (TAC) and is a Technical Program Manager at Google focused on tooling, architecture, and hardware optimization. From managing infrastructure in Singapore to advocating for cross-project clarity at the Linux Foundation, Aaron brings a uniquely strategic lens to the complexities of scaling open source silicon.</p>
